Lung Nodule Analyser

YOLO11s finds nodules on every CT slice; a 3D CNN trained on radiologists' soft labels rates each one.

Research prototype from a student project. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis. Ratings imitate LIDC-IDRI radiologists' opinions (1 = highly unlikely to 5 = highly suspicious of cancer), not biopsy results. Scans are processed on this computer only.
